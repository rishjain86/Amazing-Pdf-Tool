Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

Welcome to Amazing PDF Tool. Your privacy and the security of your documents are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application.

1. Document Processing & Security

We understand that PDF documents often contain sensitive information. Here is exactly how we process your files:

  • Local Processing: The majority of our tools (such as Merge, Split, Compress, Rotate, Add Watermark, etc.) process your files completely offline and locally on your device. These files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Cloud Processing: Advanced security features, such as Protect PDF (Encryption) and Unlock PDF (Decryption), require secure server-side processing to bypass complex AES restrictions. When you use these specific tools, your file is temporarily transmitted to our secure cloud backend.
  • Strict Deletion Policy: Any file uploaded to our cloud servers for processing is deleted immediately after the task is completed. We do not store, view, retain, or share your documents under any circumstances.

2. Advertising & Analytics

To keep the Amazing PDF Tool 100% free for everyone, we use third-party advertising services:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and use non-personally identifiable information (such as device type, IP address, and mobile advertising IDs) to provide relevant advertisements.

3. Personal Information

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal information (like your name, phone number, or address) to use the core features of the app. If you contact us for support, we will only use your email address to respond to your specific inquiry.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be updated on this page.
